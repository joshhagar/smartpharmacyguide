About the author

Josh Hagar, CPhT

Twenty years as a certified pharmacy technician, most of it at the counter where the price comes up and the conversation starts. I watched the same problems repeat: a daughter who couldn't tell the ER what her mom takes, a patient paying three copays when two would do, a refill that ran out on a Sunday. Almost none of it was medical. It was organization, and knowing what to ask. This site is everything I used to say across the counter, written down.
